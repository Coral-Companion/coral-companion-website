Features

Turn photographs into a history of individual corals

Leave tagging for hours, manually measuring corals and tedious spreadsheet magic in the past. Coral Companion does it for you.

AI Segmentation

Automatically detects and segments individual coral colonies in your survey photographs using advanced computer vision models.

Size Measurements

Automatically detects reference rulers to measure diameter and planar area.

Longitudinal Tracking

Matches colonies across separate dive sessions over months and years to track bleaching, disease, growth, and survival.
