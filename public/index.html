Live Prototype
Observe

Identify Coral

Coral Companion finds individual coral colonies in the uploaded survey image and shows you matching observations from past surveys. You can then add the image to the known coral history right away, saving you precious time.

Tested using field survey datasets from Marine Conservation Costa Rica.
Monitor

Analyse History

Compare observations of the same coral colony over time using calculated metrics of lightness and color or inspect the images visually for texture changes supported by image transformations, highlighting different aspects.

Save hours of spreadsheet magic and spend your time actually analysing actual impact of your work.
Measure

Measure Size

Coral Companion detects millimeter ticks from underwater reference rulers automatically. Computes dimensions to calculate diameter and planar area.

No more playing pixel tetris to measure your corals.
